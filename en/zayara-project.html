Self-Directed Concept Case Study · Brand Identity · Packaging · Web

Zayara — Full Creative Suite

A complete brand identity, investor pitch deck, brand guidelines, product mockups, and four coded website layouts for a premium skincare concept — designed as one coherent visual system.

01

Overview & my role

Zayara is a self-directed concept case study created to explore how a premium skincare brand can hold together across identity, packaging, investor materials, and web design.

"Zayara — Ancient Roots · Modern Science" is a concept for a Moroccan-heritage skincare line pairing argan oil, rose hip, and prickly pear with clinically dosed actives across three product lines: Origine, Aktif, and Fusion. I developed the identity, investor pitch deck, brand guidelines, product mockups, and four coded website layouts as a single end-to-end creative suite.

  • Develop a logomark, wordmark, and three colourway variants (dark, light, gold) across the full product range.
  • Build a 7-page brand guidelines document — essence, logo system, colour palette, typography, imagery direction, copy examples.
  • Produce a 9-slide investor pitch deck with market sizing, go-to-market strategy, and financial projections.
  • Render product mockup scenes for the full line, and code four distinct website layout directions.
  • Document the system so visual decisions can carry clearly across print, presentation, and digital touchpoints.

02

Project phases

  1. Phase 1

    Brand Identity System Built

    Logo, palette, typography

    A Moroccan star-lattice logomark with an oil-drop centre, a Cinzel wordmark lockup, and three approved colourways — Desert Ink (dark), Sahara Sand (light), and Argan Gold (accent) — applied across three product-line labels.

  2. Phase 2

    Guidelines, Pitch Deck & Mockups Built

    Full documentation set

    A 7-page print-ready brand guidelines PDF, a 9-slide investor pitch deck with market sizing and financial projections, and product mockup scenes covering the full Origine/Aktif/Fusion range.

  3. Phase 3

    Website Layout Directions Built

    Four coded directions

    Four distinct HTML/CSS layout concepts for the Zayara e-commerce site, from a minimal editorial direction to a warmer apothecary-style layout, each carrying the full identity system.

  4. Phase 4

    System Presentation Documented

    Cross-channel design system

    A documented creative suite showing how the identity carries across packaging, presentation materials, product visuals, and digital layouts.

03

Design brief

  • A premium, heritage-meets-science skincare identity — "ancient roots, modern science" as the core tension to resolve visually.
  • A believable, launch-ready brand across every touchpoint: identity, guidelines, pitch materials, product visuals, and a website.
  • A name and story that could support a wide product range across multiple lines and price points.

Design challenge: Create a credible premium identity that balances Moroccan heritage and modern clinical science, then carry it consistently through labels, presentation material, product visualization, and a responsive web direction.

04

Brand identity & label system

The logomark — a Moroccan six-point star lattice with an oil-drop centrepiece — carried across three approved colourways, one per product line: Origine in Argan Gold, Aktif in Desert Ink, Fusion in a deep Atlas teal.

05

Brand guidelines

The 7-page guidelines document specified the logo system, clearspace rules, the six-colour palette with Pantone and print values, and a three-font type hierarchy — everything a downstream printer, developer, or second designer would need without asking.

06

Website layout & product mockups

One of four coded layout directions — an apothecary-inspired editorial approach — plus a flat-lay mockup scene from the product visualization set, all rendered in pure HTML/CSS rather than photographed.

07

System thinking

Zayara was designed as more than a logo exercise. The same visual logic carries from the mark and label hierarchy into guidelines, presentation materials, product scenes, and a responsive website direction.

  • One visual language across touchpoints. Typography, colour, spacing, and imagery are designed to feel connected whether they appear on packaging, a deck, or a screen.
  • Production-aware details. The guidelines include clearspace, colour, and type rules that help a printer, developer, or future collaborator use the system consistently.
  • Digital direction with purpose. The HTML/CSS layouts translate the brand into a clear product and content hierarchy rather than treating the website as a separate visual exercise.
08

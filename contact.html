03 / Before you write

A few common questions.

What should a first email include?

A short description of the project, a rough timeline, and — if you have one — a budget range. Links or attachments are welcome; a finished brief is not required.

Do you take on smaller projects?

Yes. A single mark, a one-page document, or a vector cleanup is fine. If it isn't the right fit, the studio will say so and suggest someone who is.

How long does a project take?

Most engagements run four to eight weeks, from kickoff to final files. Smaller briefs land inside two weeks; complex identity work can run longer.

Do you work with clients outside Canada?

Often. The studio has shipped work to teams in the US, the UK, and across Europe. Calls are scheduled around your timezone, within reason.

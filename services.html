§ 03 / Services

What we do.

Seven disciplines, one sensibility. Each engagement begins with a written brief, a grid, and a clear scope. Projects are delivered with production files and a usage guide.

Service 01

Logo Design.

Considered marks, monograms, and logotypes drawn from first principles. Every route is developed through sketching, vector construction, and scale-testing before a recommendation is made.

Output includes a primary mark, lockup variations, and a clear usage guide covering clear-space, minimum sizes, and backgrounds — so the identity is ready to hand to printers, developers, and signage fabricators without guesswork.

Deliverables

  • Primary mark + lockups
  • Monochrome & single-colour versions
  • Vector source files (AI, SVG, PDF)
  • Usage & clear-space guide
Service 02

Graphic Design.

Campaigns, collateral, and brand-aligned visual systems across print and digital. Built on a documented grid so the work stays coherent when it's extended — by the studio or by an internal team.

Engagements typically cover posters, social templates, email layouts, and event collateral. The goal is a consistent voice across every surface, without sacrificing the specificity any one piece needs.

Deliverables

  • Print & digital collateral
  • Social & email templates
  • Campaign key visuals
  • Editable working files
Service 03

Vector & File Conversion.

Clean vector rebuilds from sketches, photos, low-resolution files, or legacy artwork. Every curve is drawn — not auto-traced — so the result holds up at any size, from a favicon to the side of a truck.

Delivered in the formats you need: layered AI or EPS for print and signage; optimised SVG for web; PDF for embedding; and flattened PNG / JPG exports at the sizes you use most.

Deliverables

  • Hand-redrawn vectors
  • AI · EPS · SVG · PDF
  • Optimised web exports
  • Print-ready CMYK files
Service 04

Technical & Architectural Graphics.

Diagrams, plans, sections, and technical illustration for the built environment. Produced with architects, engineers, and planning teams for presentations, proposals, and planning submissions.

The work is drawn to measure — line weights, hatches, and labelling systems that communicate cleanly at both A3 and on screen. Useful when a CAD export isn't readable and a photograph doesn't tell the story.

Deliverables

  • Plans, sections & elevations
  • Technical illustrations
  • Diagrams & schematics
  • Presentation-ready boards
Service 05

Layout & Web Design.

Structured, readable layouts — print-ready and web-ready. The studio designs in-browser for responsive work, on a grid for print, and documents both with the same discipline.

Typical outputs include marketing sites, portfolio sites, one-page landers, and long-form articles. Hand-off is fully specified so development stays faithful to the design.

Deliverables

  • Responsive site design
  • Component & page specs
  • Developer hand-off
  • Launch QA
Service 06

Document & Layout Design.

Reports, proposals, whitepapers, and long-form documents built on a disciplined grid. The work balances dense information with room to breathe — so the reader finishes the page instead of skimming it.

Templates are delivered alongside the finished piece, with paragraph, character, and table styles set up so the document can be updated internally without the structure drifting.

Deliverables

  • Reports & whitepapers
  • Pitch decks & proposals
  • InDesign / Figma templates
  • Style sheets & master pages
Service 07

Custom Graphic Design.

One-off, considered work for specific briefs and audiences — the projects that don't fit cleanly into a single discipline. Invitations, packaging, exhibition graphics, livery, and the occasional album sleeve.

Scoped as a bespoke engagement from the first call. Bring the problem; the studio will define the shape the solution needs to take.

Deliverables

  • Bespoke brief & scope
  • Concept routes
  • Production artwork
  • Handover documentation

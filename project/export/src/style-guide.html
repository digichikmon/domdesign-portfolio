06 / Voice

How the studio writes.

  • —Short, declarative sentences. No marketing adjectives.
  • —Titles are topic noun-phrases, not punchlines.
  • —Italics are used sparingly, for emphasis on a single word.
  • —Numbers use the em-dash range form: 2022 — 2026.
  • —No emoji, no exclamation marks, no drop-caps.
